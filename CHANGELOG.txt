Knox Buildworks - UI, Accessibility & Performance Update

[i]A major catalogue update focused on readability, faster navigation and better support for different resolutions and font sizes.[/i]

[b]Catalogue and compact mode[/b]
[list]
[*]Reworked the catalogue layout for larger fonts and smaller window sizes.
[*]The window now opens at a more practical size, while compact and detailed modes remember their own dimensions.
[*]Added a compact mode with a condensed icon grid, B41-inspired subcategory list and grouped-buildable carousel.
[*]Compact hover tooltips now show descriptions, item icons, requirements and storage information.
[/list]

[b]Buildable information[/b]
[list]
[*]Buildable dimensions are now displayed below the object preview.
[*]Storage capacity is shown in the UI.
[*]Multi-tile and multi-container objects list each available container and its capacity separately.
[/list]

[b]Appearance and accessibility[/b]
[list]
[*]Added a dedicated appearance menu with multiple UI themes, panel opacity and high-contrast options.
[*]Added checkerboard, light, dark and custom-colour backgrounds to improve the visibility of buildable sprites.
[*]Improved responsive positioning, text wrapping, scrolling and panel sizing throughout the catalogue and Planning Mode.
[/list]

[b]Detailed-list performance[/b]
[list]
[*]Greatly reduced freezing when opening the detailed list, selecting buildables and resizing the window.
[*]Added cached text layouts, native text wrapping, lazy icon loading and only-visible row processing.
[*]Detailed rows now respect the selected preview background and wrap long text correctly.
[/list]

[b]Translations and general fixes[/b]
[list]
[*]Shortened and improved English and Simplified Chinese (thank you @唐小驴) buildable descriptions.
[*]Moved shared category, subcategory and description translations into the main mod for add-on reuse.
[*]Fixed numerous scrollbar, resize-handle, carousel, selector, clipping and large-font overlap issues.
[*]Added military crates (with a capacity of 100)
[*]Fixed Double Wrought Iron Gate needing a door frame
[/list]

[i]Thank you to everyone who reported accessibility, scaling and catalogue usability issues.[/i]
