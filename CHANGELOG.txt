[b]Timber Framing, Working Fixtures & Wall Connections Update[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Wall-frame construction:[/b] Corrected wooden and metal frame connection sprites so joined frames use their complete V-shaped corner while offset joins use the separate gap-filling post. Frame corners no longer disappear into a post or attempt to attach an incompatible native entity configuration.
[*][b]T-junction walls and fences:[/b] Joining a wall or fence onto a run that continues past the junction no longer merges the two edges into a single corner piece. Wall frames, big wire fences and every other buildable with a corner sprite now appear as placed at a T-section instead of consuming both the new segment and part of the existing run.
[*][b]Vanilla wall and fence corners restored:[/b] Every entity-backed vanilla wall, fence, door frame and window frame treated its script's corner sprite as a joined corner piece, replacing both meeting edges with a single post. That sprite is the base game's gap filler for walls that meet diagonally, so it now fills the diagonal square as the base game does and leaves both walls standing. The wooden and metal wall frames keep their dedicated V-shaped joined corner.
[*][b]Materials carried in bags:[/b] Entity-backed recipes such as the Double Door no longer report planks, hinges or doorknobs as unavailable when they are inside a backpack. The authoritative server pass now lists every carried container instead of falling back to the main inventory alone, so dedicated servers accept the same materials the catalogue counted.
[*][b]Metal drums, rebuilt:[/b] Player-built metal drums never filled with water. They referenced the base game's Rain Collector entity, but that entity's SpriteConfig does not list the drum sprites, so the engine skipped the whole attachment and the drum was left without the FluidContainer component that B42 uses for rain. Buildworks now creates and configures that component itself. A single barrel also serves as either a rain collector or a burn barrel: a context-menu option switches between the two and swaps the object for the matching kind, so each mode shows only the options that belong to it instead of stacking the fluid and fireplace menus on top of each other. The Buildworks options sit directly under the drum's own context entry, and because the base game has no way at all to take fireplace fuel back out, a Dump Out Fuel option is provided. Switching refuses only while the drum still holds water or a live fire. A collecting drum shows its water sprite as it fills, and a burning one picks up the flame overlay the base game already reserves for metal drums. Both drums moved from Survival to Outdoors > Water Collectors on their correct grey and orange sprites instead of sharing one, and the welding recipe is normalised to a single sensible cost. Added the sealed metal drum as well: it holds no water and cannot be lit, but it is placed as a moveable so it can be picked back up and spent on a Charcoal Burner.
[*][b]All four generators:[/b] Only the red generator could be built. The old, blue and yellow units are now buildable too. They are not reskins: the base game reads sound radius and wear rate off the item behind the generator, so the yellow unit is the quietest and slowest to wear out, the red one is close behind, the blue frame is the lightest to carry but wears out fastest, and the old unit is the loudest and nearly as short lived. Their recipes are priced against exactly that, and all four are now a serious build: welding alongside electrics, engine parts for the better three, and up to 16 wire, 32 electronics scrap and 8 sheet metal for the yellow one.
[*][b]Church set:[/b] Added a Furniture > Church group with the two-tile altar, the lectern in all four facings, the organ stool and a full three-tile pipe organ, plus the three evangelical banners under Decorations > Banners. The organ is the first Buildworks object to stand two levels tall and is priced as a master carpenter's project.
[*][b]Brazier and log railing:[/b] Added the stone brazier under Survival > Fireplace, which takes fuel and lights like any other fireplace and picks up the base game's own fuelled and lit overlays. Added the log railing under Fencing > Railing: sections that meet join into the V-shaped corner piece on a single tile, and ends that meet offset get the free-standing pole dropped into the gap.
[*][b]Log Cabin Door Frame:[/b] The log cabin door frame is now a real door frame. It no longer registers as a solid wall edge, so players can walk through the doorway and hang a door in it as with every other door frame.
[*][b]New Structural category:[/b] Added 31 timber framing buildables covering the whole unused carpentry_03 tile sheet: plain, short, braced, reverse-braced and double-braced posts; beams, stubs, blocks and beam corners at both floor and ceiling height; small, medium, large and gable trusses; small, medium, tall and trestle frames; four heights of railing; and two cradle brackets. Posts, beams and their corners carry all four rotations, trusses, frames and railings carry both, and beams stay walk-through so they can run across a floor or overhead without blocking anyone. Framing pieces can be combined freely on a single tile, so a post, its brace and the beam it carries all share one square, in Planning Mode as well as direct build. A post no longer seals its tile against movement either. Translated into every supported language.
[*][b]Entity-backed multiplayer construction:[/b] Aligned native workstation and appliance creation with B42.20's server-side entity lifecycle. Buildworks now resolves the complete footprint before consuming materials, keeps an existing frame or wall until the replacement object is ready, and reports invalid resolved faces instead of silently consuming the recipe. This covers cases where objects such as the Pottery Bench appeared to finish without leaving a usable object.
[*][b]Safe wall and frame replacement:[/b] Building onto a planned or existing frame can no longer remove the frame first and then fail with an empty result. Solid player-built walls remain directly replaceable, while multi-tile replacement preserves the original object's position in the square.
[*][b]Garage-door stability:[/b] Garage-door sprites use the dedicated IsoDoor path so their open sprite is resolved from the native GarageDoor properties. This prevents null-sprite crashes in door toggling and the B42 lighting thread while preserving correct inside/outside orientation.
[*][b]Lighter well streaming:[/b] Well detection now checks the vanilla well sprite and hasModData before reading object mod data, avoiding empty mod-data table allocations on every unrelated object loaded with a square.
[*][b]Workshop fixtures:[/b] Added the two-tile Wooden Pegboard and Wood-Top Workbench with all four rotations, native wall/table properties, balanced carpentry and welding recipes, and translations in every supported language.
[*][b]Cleaner sprite diagnostics:[/b] Expected sprite sharing can now be declared by the definition that intentionally reuses it. Material and finish variants inside one buildable no longer generate duplicate warnings, each real cross-buildable conflict is reported only once, and moveable/prop objects no longer receive incompatible SpriteConfig components.
[*][b]Translations:[/b] Added KO translations
[/list]


[b]Planning Traffic, Construction Stability & Building Variety Update[/b]

[list]
[*][b]Consistent welding safety:[/b] Audited every JSON-defined welding recipe and added a retained welding mask directly to the definitions that were missing one.
[*][b]Functional feeding trough family:[/b] Added the missing three-tile and four-tile metal feeding troughs, with every wooden and metal size listed as its own buildable.
[*][b]Multiplayer light-switch stability:[/b] Player-built light switches now use a bounded, synchronized point light instead of unreliable map-room bounds. They can be turned on and off after chunk reloads without illuminating an entire custom structure or surrounding area.
[*][b]Cleaner pinned-recipe overlay:[/b] Reworked the overlay with a wider readable minimum size and a reserved close-button column, so long buildable names never overlap the unpin control. Pin, unpin and collapsed states now synchronize immediately in multiplayer, preventing removed pins from returning when blueprint data refreshes.
[*][b]Optional planner inventory visibility:[/b] Added a client option for keeping inventory and loot panels available in Planning Mode. Preserved panels are raised only once instead of every frame, so dragged-item icons, the cursor and tooltips remain visible; disabling the option restores the original hidden-panel behavior.
[*][b]Remote multiplayer construction:[/b] Fixed a Build 42.20 server reconstruction issue that could leave non-host players building indefinitely at 0% while the listen-server host could build normally. Remote actions now recover the authoritative server player and rebuild their native recipe context correctly.
[*][b]Reliable material checks after walking:[/b] Construction actions now rescan the containers currently reachable by the player after pathfinding. Existing plans no longer keep an outdated container list or repeatedly report missing materials until the player manually moves closer and retries each piece.
[*][b]Entity and console stability:[/b] Added validation before native entity components are attached to a sprite. Incompatible SpriteConfig combinations are skipped and reported only once, preventing repeated Invalid SpriteConfig warnings and reducing the object desynchronization that could lead to null ObjectModData packets.
[*][b]Planning traffic overhaul:[/b] Removed movement-driven blueprint requests entirely. Clients now receive their plans during the normal login handshake and request a refresh only when Planning Mode is opened; the server also verifies definition integrity, respects disabled planning and rate-limits requests. Players who never use planning no longer generate BPRequest traffic while walking or driving.
[*][b]Disabled-planning cleanup:[/b] Planning buttons now visibly respect the server setting. Attempting to use them cancels the cursor, clears stale active plans and highlights, and prevents disabled plans from leaving persistent selector lines in the world after reconnecting.
[*][b]Loose gather-zone materials:[/b] Gather zones containing only items dropped directly on the ground now work without requiring an otherwise empty crate or container in the selected area.
[*][b]Optional Menu Dock:[/b] Added a Show Menu Dock option for hiding Knox Buildworks administration and debug shortcuts from ElyonLib's Menu Dock.
[*][b]Non-blocking wall decorations:[/b] Wall plants, ivy, flowers and similar attached decorations now use collision-free moveable-object placement instead of creating an invisible impassable tile in front of the wall.
[*][b]Debug Tile Browser compatibility:[/b] Removed startup dependencies on B42.20 server-only cursor files. Its placement cursor is now created only during an active game, eliminating the failed require warning and the derive of non-table startup error.
[*][b]Localization warning fix:[/b] Reworded the Water Well initial-fill sandbox option in every supported language so literal percent text is no longer misread as a format placeholder.
[*][b]Stair chaining:[/b] Stairs can now continue from the landing of an existing flight like the vanilla building tools. Rotated stair footprints also route the builder to the actual lower step instead of an incorrect positive-axis offset.
[*][b]Safer upper-level previews:[/b] Multi-tile build cursors no longer reconnect and recalculate the same empty upper-storey squares every rendered frame. Preview preparation now runs only after the cursor moves or rotates, reducing contention with Build 42.20's asynchronous lighting updates near stairs, floors and floodlights.
[*][b]Reliable Favorites and Recent:[/b] Catalogue favorites and recent selections now synchronize their player data immediately in multiplayer instead of being vulnerable to a later server copy restoring stale entries.
[*][b]Expanded roofs, log construction and fireplace:[/b] Added six complete four-direction wooden roof slopes, four missing light-brown roof sections, a full log-cabin set with walls, corners, frames, ends and eight sloped pieces, three grey-brick fireplace-jamb pieces and seven chimney styles. Wooden slope faces are combined correctly instead of appearing as separate reverse recipes.
[*][b]More wall storage:[/b] Added eight kitchen wall cabinets plus hardwood, metal, lightwood, darkwood and white wall shelves. Every cabinet and shelf remains a separate buildable and follows its tile's native wall attachment and container properties.
[*][b]Expanded storage, shop fixtures and microwaves:[/b] Added 40 more missing counters, corner and overhead cabinets, wall shelves, lockers, crates, tool storage, restaurant displays, bookcases, multi-tile wardrobes, magazine racks, cooled display shelves and a large log stack. Added white and chrome microwaves as functional appliances.
[*][b]Wooden-panel fixtures:[/b] Added the matching wooden-panel door frame, window frame and passable pillar, together with the missing plain wooden window.
[*][b]Vanilla-matched gravel:[/b] Remapped gravel floors, corners and edges to the base game's gravel palette so new paths blend naturally into existing vanilla gravel.
[*][b]Wrought Iron Gate:[/b] Added the missing single-tile Wrought Iron Gate with correct west/north closed sprites, matching open sprites, metalworking requirements and functional door collision.
[*][b]Correct wall and fence connections:[/b] Matching north/west pieces placed on the same tile now merge into their V-shaped corner sprite, while offset perpendicular ends use a dedicated pillar where one exists. Connections are restricted to the same buildable, stage, material and variant, preventing unrelated nearby objects from creating incorrect corners.
[*][b]Fireplace alignment:[/b] Corrected the west/north sprite mapping for single and double fireplaces so they render on the proper side of brick jambs instead of being hidden behind them.
[*][b]Buildables balance:[/b] Removed glass pane req from Bank Wall Frames.
[*][b]Translations:[/b] Added FR, PTBR, RO translations
[/list]



[b]Build Smarter, Rebuild Faster - Catalogue Visibility & Multiplayer Reliability Update[/b]

[list]
[*][b]Multi-category browsing:[/b] Categories can now be combined with Shift-click, making it possible to browse related sets together, for example walls, doors and windows, while filters automatically include the combined selection.
[*][b]Larger icons and focused previews:[/b] Added Default, Large and Extra Large buildable-icon sizes for both the normal catalogue and Compact mode. Added optional hover previews beneath each favorite star, so previews appear only when deliberately requested. Compact previews include descriptions, capacities, materials, tools and skill requirements; normal and detailed-list previews show only the enlarged buildable. Hover previews are disabled by default and have their own size setting.
[*][b]Improved gathering and build queues:[/b] Gather zones now recognize loose items placed directly on the ground as well as items inside crates, bags, nearby containers and vehicles. Pinned requirement totals include those world items, and queued plans can walk to and collect them automatically.
[*][b]Multiplayer definition validation:[/b] Added a safe retry for the B42.20 client/server definition handshake, preventing a dropped initial response from leaving players permanently stuck on "Waiting for server definition validation."
[*][b]Pinned overlay controls:[/b] Added an unpin control to every pinned recipe and blueprint header, so obsolete entries can be removed directly from the overlay. Deleted blueprints are also detected and cleared automatically.
[*][b]Furnace and charcoal-burner placement:[/b] Blast, primitive and smelting furnaces, together with all charcoal-burner variants, now follow the base game's outdoor-only restriction. The restriction is validated by the definition schema, displayed in compact information and produces a clear placement reason indoors.
[*][b]Classic brick structures:[/b] Restored the Classic Brick Wall to catalogue searches and grouped it after Brick Wall (Poor), with its old and weathered appearance available through the finish selector. Poor brick walls, door frames and window frames no longer incorrectly offer plaster, paint or wallpaper finishes.
[*][b]Cleaner logging:[/b] Routine Knox Buildworks debug and information output is now disabled during normal play. Full diagnostics remain available through the Debug option, while genuine errors continue to be logged.
[*][b]Wall replacement:[/b] Finished player-built walls can now be replaced directly by another solid wall on the same edge. Door and window frames, openings, fences and map-authored walls remain protected, so changing a wall style no longer requires destroying and rebuilding its frame.
[*][b]Passable pillars and posts:[/b] Fixed the directional invisible collision created by wall pillars, tall posts and other explicitly passable decorative pieces. They now use collision-free world objects and remain walkable in every rotation, including sprites that are not registered as movable furniture.
[*][b]Magazine or skill progression:[/b] Learned construction recipes can now accept a skill threshold as an alternative to finding their magazine. Native B42 AutoLearn rules are preserved, required construction skills are used where available, and Metal Floor can be unlocked at Welding 1. Recipe-or-skill conditions are shown throughout the catalogue, detailed information, pinned recipes and requirement drawers.
[*][b]Metal rain drum:[/b] Added the four-direction base-game Metal Drum as a functional rainwater collector in Vanilla Expanded. It uses the native fluid-container system for multiplayer-safe water storage.
[*][b]Vanilla-style menu controls:[/b] Escape now closes the Knox Buildworks catalogue and its standalone guide, appearance, pinned-recipe, blueprint and administration windows, matching the base-game building and crafting menus.
[*][b]Localization formatting:[/b] Corrected parameterized translations to use numbered placeholders and direct getText arguments. Removed the blueprint UI's fallback-based safeText wrappers so translated strings now follow the base-game localization path consistently. This is due to B42.20.2 update.
[*][b]Two-sided frame construction:[/b] Walls can now be completed from a matching frame while the player stands on either usable face or the adjacent approach tile, instead of being routed through or around the frame to its anchor square. This also applies when building walls from plans and queues.
[*][b]Upper-storey perimeter walls:[/b] Walls, wall frames, door frames and window frames can now use a floor on either side of their placement edge, so south and east exterior walls no longer require a temporary floor outside the building.
[/list]


[b]Knox Buildworks - Build Rules & Appliances Update[/b]

[list]
[*][b]Build 42.20 multiplayer XP:[/b] Fixed XP awards for entity-backed buildables. Knox Buildworks now verifies the native CraftRecipe award, applies a safe fallback when Build 42.20 skips it, avoids duplicate XP and displays the normal crafting XP halo above the player.
[*][b]New server-authoritative Build Rules Editor:[/b] Added a new administration UI to the ElyonLib dock. Admins can enable, disable or inherit rules by category, subcategory and individual buildable, then customize each build stage's materials, tools, accepted items or tags, item-use behavior, skills, XP, learned recipes and construction time. Rules are validated, revision-checked, stored by the server and synchronized to every client, with the catalogue, pinned recipes, planning tools and construction all respecting the active settings.
[*][b]Rule presets and sharing:[/b] Added Current Server Rules, Definition Defaults and Disable All starting points, plus named client-side preset import and export. Presets can be backed up, shared and moved between servers without changing a server until an admin validates and saves them.
[*][b]Wall-finish policies:[/b] Added a Finishes tab where admins can make plaster, paint or wallpaper requirements free for all walls, then set individual walls to inherit, require or waive each cost. Non-wall paintable objects, including military crates, retain their normal paint requirements.
[*][b]Admin UI and performance:[/b] Reworked the preset area for clearer navigation and virtualized large scrolling lists to greatly improve performance in the Rules Editor, Test Runner and Tile Browser. Added distinct ElyonLib dock icons for all three tools.
[*][b]New in-game guide:[/b] Added a Survival Guide-style Knox Buildworks manual, opened from new information buttons. It covers the catalogue, variants, requirements, building, painting, functional objects, pinned recipes, planning, queues, sharing, accessibility, multiplayer troubleshooting and the complete admin workflow.
[*][b]Accessibility option:[/b] Added a toggle to Knox UI Appearance that prevents unavailable buildable icons from fading. Readiness indicators and building restrictions remain unchanged.
[*][b]Localization overhaul:[/b] Added full Spanish and Russian localization to both Knox Buildworks and Vanilla Expanded, completed and synchronized the English, Simplified Chinese, Spanish and Russian key sets, removed stray translations and centralized shared category and subcategory names in the main mod for add-on reuse.
[*][b]New functional appliances and bathroom fixtures:[/b] Added 36 buildables to Vanilla Expanded, including ovens, a wood stove, charcoal and propane BBQs, refrigerators and freezers, washing machines and dryers, toilets, a shower, a bathtub and several sinks. Matching styles are grouped for easier browsing, recipes use balanced welding, electrical and plumbing requirements.
[*][b]General and buildable fixes:[/b] Improved editor validation and error feedback, corrected the Large White Window name, allowed it to be installed in compatible passable interior arches without turning those walls into window frames, removed the obsolete tile-property patches, and tightened catalogue, pinned-recipe, planning and placement behavior around live rule changes.
[*][b]Buildables definitions:[/b] Removed the glass panels from the definitions of some window frames.
[/list]
