title=[B42] Knox Buildworks
description=[h1]Knox Buildworks[/h1]
description=[i]Plan it. See it. Build it.[/i]
description=
description=Knox Buildworks is a complete Build 42 construction framework and catalogue. It organizes thousands of recipes, explains what each one needs, supports grouped variants and finishes, and adds persistent blueprints for projects of any size.
description=
description=[quote]
description=[b]Build 42 only[/b] | [b]Single-player and multiplayer[/b] | [b]Requires ElyonLib[/b]
description=
description=Enable [b]Knox Buildworks[/b] for the framework and converted construction. Enable the included [b]Knox Buildworks - Vanilla Expanded[/b] add-on for the much larger collection of Build 42 tiles.
description=Build 42.21 compatibility: furniture and props use the updated placement API. The catalogue opens when definitions finish loading if F7 is pressed early.
description=[/quote]
description=
description=[h2]A catalogue built for a large collection[/h2]
description=Open Knox with [b]F7[/b] or from the Building Menu drawer. Categories and subcategories stay visible beside the buildable list. Search by name, required item, or everything; filter by material and skill; sort alphabetically; show every construction quality; or progressively filter to recipes that are ready now.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Grid, list, and compact views[/b] with saved window size and position.
description=[*][b]Favorites, recent recipes, and pins[/b] for things you use often.
description=[*][b]Grouped recipes[/b] for related qualities, shapes, models, materials, colours, and finishes.
description=[*][b]Explicit magnifier previews[/b] in normal view and full detail tooltips in compact view.
description=[*][b]Clear readiness borders[/b] without covering every available card in bright green.
description=[*][b]World lookup actions[/b] to find a recipe for an existing piece or compatible doors and windows for an opening.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Understand the recipe before placing it[/h2]
description=The fixed inspector keeps the buildable preview and choices beside [b]Materials & Tools[/b] and [b]Skills & Knowledge[/b]. Counts show what you have and what you need. Select a requirement to open Available Ingredients / Possible Items on the far right, see every accepted alternative, and choose the exact input when necessary.
description=
description=Rows explain whether an item is consumed, retained, drained by uses, destroyed, or allowed to degrade. Storage capacity, dimensions, time, light, learned recipes, and other useful properties appear before construction begins.
description=
description=[h2]Direct construction[/h2]
description=Choose a family and its quality, variant, material, or finish, then select [b]Build[/b]. The cursor rotates and snaps according to the object, validates the complete footprint, and tells you why a location is invalid. Compatible recipes stay on the cursor for repeated placement while supplies remain available. Right-click cancels placement and returns to the catalogue cleanly.
description=
description=Knox reserves a valid set of ingredients before consuming anything, keeps required tools, awards configured XP, and creates the appropriate world object. The server validates the same choice again in multiplayer.
description=
description=[h2]Planning Mode[/h2]
description=Planning uses two focused windows: the blueprint workspace on the left and a compact planning catalogue on the right. Search and Category/Subcategory selectors narrow the grid; arrow controls cycle grouped pieces, variants, materials, and finishes without replacing the workspace with the main catalogue.
description=
description=Create independent plans, draw named rooms, choose Z levels, and place non-blocking ghosts. [b]Preview layout[/b] opens a zoomable isometric or top-down view with floor navigation, a grid, rooms, and the supply zone. The [b]Supplies[/b] tab allocates shared ingredients across the plan and reports Have / Need totals from inventory, ground, gather containers, and vehicles. Build one ready piece or queue the plan.
description=
description=Blueprints remember direction and every selected choice. Move, edit, remove a selected pending piece, export, import, or write plans to an in-game blueprint item.
description=
description=[b]Planning Mode in action[/b]
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/i5lLGS4.gif[/img]
description=
description=[h2]Walls, openings, and functional objects[/h2]
description=Knox follows Build 42 placement rules for floors, wall edges, frames, support, distance, collisions, and multi-tile footprints. Compatible windows and doors are matched to their opening shape. Wall details such as trims are attached to the wall itself to avoid overlap flicker and can be removed from the world menu.
description=Single-tile walls record materials for vanilla dismantling.
description=
description=Supported storage, doors, windows, native light switches, appliances, work surfaces, furnaces, and wells keep their gameplay behavior after construction. Limited wells use server capacity and refill rules instead of becoming unlimited.
description=
description=[h2]Personal appearance settings[/h2]
description=The catalogue gear updates the open UI while you change it. Choose among seven panel tones, high contrast, 35-100% panel opacity, 75-175% card size, three preview sizes, faded or full-strength unavailable icons, and checkerboard, light, dark, or custom preview backgrounds. These choices are saved locally and do not change server rules.
description=
description=[h2]Server controls[/h2]
description=Sandbox options cover the primary Building Menu action, Planning Mode, blueprint size and radius, construction XP, paint requirements, and infinite or limited wells. The Rules Editor, accesible by admins or in debug from the Menu Dock (added by ElyonLib) can control availability and override stage inputs, skills, XP, knowledge, finishes, and time. Blueprint owners can grant view, contribute or build access to factions and individual players.
description=
description=[h2]Existing saves and removal[/h2]
description=Knox can be added to an existing save, but make a complete backup while the game or server is stopped first. Server and clients must enable matching versions of ElyonLib, Knox Buildworks, and every selected add-on.
description=
description=[b]Do not remove Knox or an enabled construction add-on from a save that has loaded them.[/b] Custom items and native entities receive persistent WorldDictionary identities. Removing their mod can prevent the world from loading even after placed objects are deleted. Restore the original mod set or a pre-installation backup; deleting WorldDictionary files is not an uninstall method.
description=
description=[h2]Vanilla Expanded[/h2]
description=The bundled optional add-on turns the Build 42 tile library into an organized building collection: walls, roofs, doors, windows, floors, fences, containers, furniture, architectural details, decorations, road pieces, vegetation, and survival structures. Enable the focused core alone or add the full collection.
