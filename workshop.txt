title=[B42] Knox Buildworks
description=[h1]Knox Buildworks[/h1]
description=[i]Plan it. See it. Build it.[/i]
description=
description=Knox Buildworks is a complete Build 42 construction framework and catalogue. It organizes thousands of recipes, explains what each one needs, supports grouped variants and finishes, and adds persistent blueprints with a roof helper for projects of any size.
description=
description=[quote]
description=[b]Build 42 only[/b] | [b]Single-player and multiplayer[/b] | [b]Requires ElyonLib[/b]
description=
description=Enable [b]Knox Buildworks[/b] for the framework, converted construction, building components and magazines. Enable the included [b]Knox Buildworks - Vanilla Expanded[/b] add-on for the much larger collection of Build 42 tiles.
description=[/quote]
description=
description=[h2]A catalogue built for a large collection[/h2]
description=Open Knox with [b]F7[/b] or from the Building Menu drawer. Search by name or required item, filter by category, material and skill, show every construction quality, or narrow the list to recipes that are ready now.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Grid, list, and compact views[/b] with saved window size and position.
description=[*][b]Favorites, recent recipes, and pins[/b] for things you use often.
description=[*][b]Grouped recipes[/b] for qualities, shapes, materials, colours, and finishes.
description=[*][b]World lookup[/b] finds the recipe for an existing piece or the doors and windows that fit an opening.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Understand the recipe before placing it[/h2]
description=The inspector keeps the preview beside [b]Materials & Tools[/b] and [b]Skills & Knowledge[/b], with what you have against what you need. Select a requirement to see every accepted alternative and choose the exact input. Rows show whether an item is consumed, kept, drained or may degrade, plus storage, time, light and learned recipes.
description=
description=[h2]Direct construction[/h2]
description=Choose a family and its quality, variant, material or finish, then [b]Build[/b]. The cursor rotates and snaps, validates the whole footprint and tells you why a spot is invalid. Knox reserves ingredients before consuming anything, keeps tools, awards XP and creates the proper world object; the server checks the same choice in multiplayer.
description=
description=[h2]Magazines and building components[/h2]
description=17 magazines - Fine Woodworking, The Family Handyman, Popular Mechanics and more - each unlock a group of recipes; a high enough skill unlocks them too. Refrigerant, compressors, plastic sheets, insulation, rubber tubing, copper pipe and rubber seals supply appliances and plumbing.
description=
description=[h2]Planning Mode[/h2]
description=The blueprint workspace sits on the left and a compact planning catalogue on the right; Search and Category/Subcategory selectors narrow its grid, and arrows cycle pieces, variants, materials and finishes.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Plans and rooms[/b] on any level; extend or cut rooms into any shape.
description=[*][b]Quickbar[/b] slots for saved pieces, and a [b]pipette[/b] to plan more of a ghost or built piece without searching.
description=[*][b]Select[/b] and erase ghosts, move the whole plan, and set tint, opacity and visible levels.
description=[*][b]Preview layout[/b] in isometric or top-down view.
description=[*][b]Supplies[/b] totals from inventory, bags, the ground, gather containers and vehicles; build one piece or queue the plan.
description=[/list]
description=Plans can be exported, imported, written to an in-game blueprint item and shared with faction members or players.
description=
description=[h2]Roof Helper[/h2]
description=Select a room and the helper lists every roof that fits it - flat, shed, gable, hip and cross-gable, at 30 or 45 degrees, over L, T, U or irregular rooms - as small 3D models, best first. Hover to see it in the world, then add it to the plan with gable walls in any variant and paint, plaster or wallpaper.
description=
description=[b]Planning Mode in action[/b]
description=[img]https://i.imgur.com/i5lLGS4.gif[/img]
description=
description=[h2]Walls, openings, and functional objects[/h2]
description=Knox follows Build 42 rules for floors, wall edges, frames, support and multi-tile footprints. Windows and doors match their opening shape, trims attach to the wall, and walls record materials for vanilla dismantling. Storage, doors, windows, glass, curtains, light switches, appliances, furnaces and wells keep their gameplay behavior.
description=
description=[h2]Personal appearance settings[/h2]
description=The catalogue gear changes the open UI live: seven panel tones, high contrast, 35-100% opacity, 75-175% card size, three preview sizes and several preview backgrounds. Saved locally; server rules are unaffected.
description=
description=[h2]Server controls[/h2]
description=Sandbox options cover the Building Menu action, Planning Mode, blueprint size and radius, XP, paint requirements and wells. The Rules Editor (admins or debug, from the ElyonLib Menu Dock) controls availability and overrides inputs, skills, XP, knowledge, finishes and time.
description=
description=[h2]Existing saves and removal[/h2]
description=Knox can be added to an existing save; back it up first with the game or server stopped. Server and clients need matching versions of ElyonLib, Knox Buildworks and every enabled add-on.
description=
description=[b]Do not remove Knox or an enabled construction add-on from a save that has loaded them.[/b] Custom items and native entities receive persistent WorldDictionary identities, and removing their mod can stop the world from loading. Restore the original mod set or a backup; deleting WorldDictionary files is not an uninstall method.
description=
description=[h2]Vanilla Expanded[/h2]
description=The optional add-on turns the Build 42 tile library into an organized collection: walls, roofs, doors, windows, floors, fences, containers, furniture, details, decorations, road pieces, vegetation and survival structures.
description=
