title=[B42] Knox Buildworks
description=[h1]Knox Buildworks[/h1]
description=[i]Plan it. See it. Build it.[/i]
description=
description=Knox Buildworks is a complete building experience. It brings construction into one searchable catalogue, makes recipes easier to understand, supports real multi-tile structures and wall finishes, and adds a persistent blueprint planner for projects of any size.
description=
description=It is independent from the vanilla Building Menu and can safely live beside it. Use Knox when you want it, keep the original menu available, or let a server make Knox the primary building action.
description=
description=[quote]
description=[b]Build 42 only[/b] | [b]Single-player and multiplayer[/b] | [b]Requires ElyonLib[/b]
description=
description=Enable [b]Knox Buildworks[/b] for the framework and converted Build 42 construction. Enable the included [b]Knox Buildworks - Vanilla Expanded[/b] mod as well for the much larger, carefully organized building collection.
description=[/quote]
description=
description=[h2]Everything in its place[/h2]
description=Open the catalogue with [b]F7[/b] or from the Building Menu drawer on the left. Search by recipe name or required item, browse in grid or list view, and filter by category, subcategory, material or skill. Favourites, recent recipes and pinned recipes keep the things you actually build close at hand.
description=
description=Construction qualities that belong together appear as one family instead of separate Level 1, Level 2 and Level 3 cards. Model variants, colours and material choices are grouped the same way, and your selection carries into blueprint placement.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]More than 1,700 buildable definitions[/b] across the core and Vanilla Expanded collection.
description=[*][b]Clear requirements[/b] for materials, tools, uses, alternatives, skills, recipes, time and light.
description=[*][b]Directional and multi-tile placement[/b] for walls, floors, stairs, gates and irregular objects.
description=[*][b]Plaster, paint, wallpaper and signs[/b] using the correct tools, colour, pattern and wall shape.
description=[*][b]Persistent blueprints[/b] with rooms, Z levels, ghosts, gather areas, totals and build queues.
description=[*][b]Multiplayer integrity[/b] with synchronized plans and server-side validation.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Know what the recipe will use[/h2]
description=Materials and tools are separate from skills and knowledge, so a missing hammer never looks like a missing Carpentry level. Recipe rows explain whether an item is consumed, kept, drained by uses, destroyed or allowed to degrade.
description=
description=Select a requirement to see [b]Available Ingredients / Possible Items[/b], including what you own and every valid alternative. You can choose the specific item to use, or pin the recipe to a configurable HUD tracker with icons or text.
description=
description=[h2]Build naturally[/h2]
description=Knox placement follows Build 42 rules for floors, wall edges, frames, previous stages, collisions, distance, support and footprints. Compatible objects remain on the cursor for repeated placement while materials are available, and native entity-backed objects retain their specialized game behaviour.
description=
description=Build supported walls bare or plan their plaster, exact paint colour or wallpaper in advance. Standalone finish actions can also target compatible existing walls. Knox only shows finishes supported by that surface.
description=
description=[h2]Plan the whole project first[/h2]
description=Planning Mode lets you design before spending materials. Create independent blueprints, draw named and coloured rooms, choose a Z level, and place non-blocking ghosts with their direction, quality, variant, material and finish remembered.
description=
description=Draw walls in lines and floors in areas while large objects remain safe single placements. Floors, walls, furniture and finishes can share a planned tile, and plans can be moved, edited or erased later.
description=
description=A [b]gather area[/b] can include world containers and vehicle storage. Review whole-project totals, build one ghost, or queue the plan. Blueprints can be exported as JSON, imported at a new origin, or written onto an in-game blueprint item.
description=
description=[h2]Designed for multiplayer[/h2]
description=The server rechecks buildable choices, requirements, skills, recipes, finishes, position and permissions. Integrity checks prevent mismatched clients from building with incompatible definitions. Blueprint owners can grant [b]view[/b], [b]contribute[/b] or [b]build[/b] access to their faction or individual players.
description=
description=Server options cover Planning Mode, the primary Building Menu action, blueprint limits, construction XP, and infinite or limited wells that refill from groundwater and rain.
description=
description=[h2]Vanilla Expanded[/h2]
description=The bundled Vanilla Expanded add-on turns the Build 42 tile library into a practical catalogue. Walls are grouped by family, quality, model and available colour, alongside a broad selection of roofs, doors, windows, floors, fences, containers, furniture, architectural details, decorations, road pieces, vegetation and survival structures. It remains optional, so you can enable the focused core or the full collection.
description=
description=[h2]For add-on creators[/h2]
description=Knox is also a data-driven framework. Add-ons can define templates, material groups, stages, variants, multi-level geometry, recipes, skills and wall finishes through JSON, while still referencing native entities when engine components are needed.
description=
